## Notas de Diseño

### Brand Colors
- Primary: Terracota (#E07A5F)
- Secondary: Stone (#F4F1DE)
- Accent: Emerald (#81B29A)
- Dark: Charcoal (#3D405B)

### Fonts
- Headlines: Bold, sans-serif
- Body: Regular, legible on mobile
- Keep text concise: max 2-3 lines per slide

### Visual Style
- Real photos over stock (Barcelona authentic)
- Overlays with slight opacity for text readability
- Consistent icon style through all carousels

### Posting Schedule
- Post carousel + reel same day
- Best time: 19:00-21:00 España
- Add to highlights after 24h

### Templates
Create templates in Canva with:
- Locked fonts/colors
- Placeholder for images
- Text boxes pre-formatted
- CTA slide customizable per topic
